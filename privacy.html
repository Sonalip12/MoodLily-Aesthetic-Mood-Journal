Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, and encrypted password.
  • Wellness content you add: mood logs and journal entries.
  • Technical data: basic server logs, session cookies, and diagnostics for security.

How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To save your mood and journal history across devices and sessions.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix reliability issues.

Storage and security

MoodLily stores app data in a managed cloud database and uses secure transport (HTTPS) for network traffic. Passwords are never stored as plain text.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only use infrastructure providers required to run MoodLily, such as hosting, database, and monitoring services.

Your choices

You can request account data updates or deletion by contacting support. We respond as quickly as possible and typically within 3 business days.
