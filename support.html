Support

Response window: typically within 24 to 72 hours

Contact channels

  • Email: support@moodlily.app
  • Subject format: [MoodLily] Issue type - short summary
  • Include device, browser, and steps to reproduce for faster help.

Common help topics

  • Login issues: verify you are on the active production URL and check email/password spelling.
  • Session issues: sign out and sign in again after clearing stale browser cookies.
  • Data requests: ask for account export or deletion through support email.

Security reports

If you discover a vulnerability, send details to support@moodlily.app with steps and screenshots. Please avoid public disclosure until the issue is fixed.
